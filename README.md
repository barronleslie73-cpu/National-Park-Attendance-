# National Park Attendance Case Study (1998-2025)

## Project Overview
This project explores the correlation between the rise of major social media platforms and the significant surge in National Park attendance. By cleaning and analyzing historical data, this study visualizes how digital trends impact physical foot traffic in nature.

## Parks Analyzed
* **Glacier National Park**
* **Everglades National Park**
* **Mammoth Cave National Park**
* **Wind Cave National Park**

## Tools Used
* **Data Cleaning:** R and Node.js
* **Visualizations:** Chart.js and Flourish
* **Presentation:** Google Slides

## Files in this Repository
* `combined_national_park_data.xlsx`: The raw dataset used for analysis.
* `How has media impacted the National park attendance_.pdf`: The final presentation deck.
